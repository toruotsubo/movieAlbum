About MovieAlbum

MovieAlbum is an app for managing your video files.

  • You can organize videos using tags, ratings, categories, featured flags, and more.
  • In addition to the full video index, you can create a custom index for different purposes.
  • You can set your favorite moments as thumbnail images (some video formats have limitations).
  • You can also manage selected videos in groups.

Supported file types

  • mp4
  • m4v
  • webm
  • ogv
  • ogg
  • avi*
  • wmv*

* has limitations.

Privacy Policy

  • This app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.
  • No information is shared with external servers or third parties.
